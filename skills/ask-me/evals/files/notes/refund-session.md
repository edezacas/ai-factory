# Interview: refunds

**Agent:** Who approves a refund? I recommend an operator, whatever the amount.
**User:** An operator, yes.

**Agent:** Where does the money go? I recommend the original payment method.
**User:** Yes, the original payment method.

**Agent:** What happens with a partially shipped order: refund only the lines not shipped, or hold the whole refund until the rest arrives?
**User:** Good question, let me think about that one.

**Agent:** Should the customer be notified? I recommend an email when the operator approves.
**User:** Let's get back to that partial shipment thing later.
